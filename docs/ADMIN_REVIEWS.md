# Admin Product Reviews

The Admin `Reviews` workspace is organized by product rather than by rating.

## Product list

The first view shows each product with:

- product name and ID
- assigned category or categories
- number of customer reviews
- average rating
- a `View reviews` action

Products can be searched by product name, product ID, or category name. Rating is not used as the primary filter.

## Product review detail

Selecting a product opens the reviews for that product only. The detail view shows:

- customer name and customer ID
- star rating
- written review
- posted date and review ID

Admin remains read-only for customer reviews. Review creation, editing, and deletion remain customer-owned actions.
